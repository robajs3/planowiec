You can install Planowiec like a regular app — you'll get an icon on your home screen / app drawer, and the app will open without the browser's address bar, just like a native app. Pick your platform below.

🖥️ Computer (Chrome / Edge) ⌄
1

Click the three-dot icon (⋮) in the top-right corner of the browser window.

2

Hover over "Cast, save, and share".

3

From the submenu, choose "Install Planowiec".

🤖 Android (Chrome) ⌄
1

Tap the three-dot icon (⋮) in the top-right corner of the screen.

2

From the menu, choose "Install app" (on some phones it's under "Add to Home screen").

3

Confirm with "Install Planowiec". The icon will land on your home screen / app drawer.

🍎 iPhone / iPad (Safari) ⌄

Must be opened in Safari — Chrome on iOS doesn't have the option to add to the home screen.

1

Tap the three-dot icon (⋯) in the bottom-right corner of the screen (next to the address bar).

2

Choose "Share" ⬆️.

3

From the option list, choose "Add to Home Screen" and confirm with "Add" in the top-right corner.
